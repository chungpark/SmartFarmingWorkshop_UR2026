# Organizers

## Yonghyun Kim  
**George Washington University**

Yonghyun Kim is a Ph.D. student in Biomedical Engineering at George Washington University. His interests include robotics, sensing, and intelligent systems, with emphasis on practical applications of perception and autonomous systems in real-world environments.

## Ki-Beom Lee  
**National Institute of Agricultural Sciences, Rural Development Administration, Republic of Korea**

Ki-Beom Lee is an Agricultural Researcher at the National Institute of Agricultural Sciences, Rural Development Administration. His work focuses on visual intelligence for agricultural robotics and related sensing technologies for agricultural automation.

## Chung Hyuk Park  
**George Washington University**

Chung Hyuk Park is a faculty member in the Department of Biomedical Engineering at George Washington University. His research spans machine learning, computer vision, haptics, telepresence robotics, multimodal human–robot interaction, and assistive robotics, with emphasis on real-world human-centered robotic systems.
